## 01_SplitLowRock

Use case: stylized-concept. Create ONE clean professional 3D modeling turnaround reference sheet of a game environment rock asset. Attached screenshot is ONLY art-direction and shape reference; DO NOT include screenshot, HUD, units, terrain or buildings in output. Subject: The low split elongated rock in the LOWER LEFT of the reference gameplay. A single low broad oblong rock split into two adjacent chunky masses by ONE deep asymmetric vertical fissure. Width:depth:height approximately 3:1.5:1. Flattened sloping top, blunt beveled corners, broad planar sides. Show ONLY the main split rock, no peripheral gravel. Match the approved reference rock style closely: simple chunky low-poly geometry, VERY broad smooth flat faces, muted warm gray-brown taupe stone, subtle thin golden ochre sand dust painted on upper surfaces, darker taupe sides. Rock body is gray-brown NOT bright orange sandstone. Minimal surface detail, no photorealism, no fine triangulated noise, no layered mesa, no crystals, no ornate sculpt. Easy to build with a small number of beveled polygons. Compose 2x2 balanced panels on uniform off-white background: upper left true orthographic FRONT at eye level, upper right true orthographic RIGHT SIDE at eye level, lower left true orthographic TOP straight down 90 degrees, lower right elevated three-quarter 3D view. All four depict EXACT SAME mesh geometry with consistent features, proportions, crack placement and stone count. Equal scale in orthographic views, generous margins, complete unobscured silhouette in every panel. Show full object down to flat base with no ground obscuring it, no sand piles, no ground plane mesh. Soft neutral studio lighting to reveal geometry; minimal contact shadow in perspective view only. No text, no labels, no arrows, no borders, no extra objects. Landscape high resolution polished modeling reference.
